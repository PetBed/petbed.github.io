01 / BEGIN AT THE GATE

Your first season

The tutorial walks through planting, harvesting, pressing, bottling, labeling, and making your first market sale. It resumes at the start of its current phase; resuming can reset the tutorial's Pinot Noir or tutorial wine.

New save150 gold · 3 open plots · 1 French Oak barrel
Tutorial start20 gold · 2 Pinot Noir seeds

The opening seed stock also includes two packets each of Hops and Barley and one Blackberry packet. Other seeds begin at zero. Temperate is the starting weather.

Grow the estate

There are nine plots. The first plot purchase costs 50 gold; each following price is rounded from 1.5 times the previous price. Barrel types cost 200-500 gold, with a limit of two owned per type. Oak Conditioning costs 250 gold and permanently speeds cellar aging by 30%. The Copper Kettle costs 150 gold and unlocks the Brewery.

02 / VINEYARD & PANTRY

Crops, weather & flavour

Buy a seed packet from the Merchant and plant it in an empty plot. Planting consumes one seed. Timers count down in the game loop; harvest when the plot is ready. Weather rerolls every three minutes and may repeat. At harvest, flavour-bearing grapes and berries receive that weather's modifiers, clamped to the 0-100 range.

Hold the bubble

Keep the green catcher pad over the moving bubble. Progress builds during overlap and recedes slowly outside it. Stoke Fire adds rightward velocity; Vent Steam adds leftward velocity. The pad has momentum, drag, and damped edge bounces. Switching in-game tabs does not reset the kettle; browser background-tab throttling can slow its interval.

Required overlap time7.5 + ((base price - 30) / 100) × 14.5 seconds

That is alignment time, not total elapsed time. When a brew finishes, the beer enters the reserve and its label editor opens. Beer has no wine aging or quality multiplier.

06 / TOWN TRADE

Market square

Prices shift every 30 seconds. Wine uses random volatility from 0.8x to 1.2x; beer uses 0.85x to 1.15x. Selling one unit adds one oversupply point to that exact product. At each market shift, oversupply first decays by 20%.

Oversupply penaltymax(0.3, 1 - 0.05 × oversupply)

New market price is recipe base price × volatility × oversupply penalty. The penalty floor is 0.3x. Wine sales then apply the bottle's quality and vintage multipliers. Beer sells at current market price alone. Sparklines retain up to ten prices.

07 / A CUSTOMER'S REQUEST

Customer orders

Requests specify a wine recipe and one to four flavour ranges. A solver checks that a valid combination of weather, eligible additive, and barrel can satisfy the generated request. New orders are attempted on a randomized 10-15 minute interval while fewer than ten available orders are posted; each available order expires after 20-30 minutes.

Accept up to three orders at a time. Active orders do not expire. A rejected wine remains in reserve and the order stays open; cancelling removes the order.

Match & payout

For each requested attribute, deviation is zero inside the range and otherwise the distance to the nearest range edge. The average across requested attributes must be at most 20 points on the 0-100 scale. This is an absolute point difference, not a relative percentage.

08 / MAKE IT YOURS

Labels, art & sound

Label any reserve wine or beer. Titles allow up to 22 characters, including spaces and punctuation. Choose a rectangle, oval, or shield; a none, solid, dashed, or double border; and a star, crown, grape, leaf, droplet, or diamond crest.

09 / KEEP YOUR NOTES

Saves & settings

Progress autosaves to browser local storage every ten seconds. Press Ctrl+S on Windows/Linux or Cmd+S on macOS to save manually. The save belongs to the current browser and device.

IncludedGold, stock, plots, barrels, wines, racks, beers, orders, tutorial, settings
Not includedMarket prices, price history, oversupply

Market values and oversupply return to their defaults after reload. The normal Settings modal changes the font. Dialogue difficulty is persisted but currently exposed only in the playtest controls.
