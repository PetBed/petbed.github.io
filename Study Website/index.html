How to get your Google Calendar secret iCal link ▼

1. On computer, open Google Calendar.

2. On the left under "My calendars", hover over your calendar, click the 3 dots ⋮ → Settings and sharing.

3. Scroll down to the "Integrate calendar" section.

4. Copy the URL in the box labeled "Secret address in iCal format" (or "Public address in iCal format").
